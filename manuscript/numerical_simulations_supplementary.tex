\section{Additional results}
\label{eq:additional_results}

In the present supplementary material, we compare the approximated total-field anomaly (\textbf{atfa}), 
amplitude of magnetic induction field (\textbf{b}), RTP anomaly (\textbf{rtp}), $x$, $y$ and $z$ 
components of the magnetic induction field (\textbf{bx}, \textbf{by} and \textbf{bz}) 
produced by the estimated equivalent layers shown in Figure \parttworef{fig:Figure4} and those produced by the 
simulated magnetic source (Figure \parttworef{fig:Figure2}). The fields produced by the equivalent layers are 
referred as ``predicted fields''. Those produced by the magnetic source are referred as 
``simulated fields''. The simulated fields are those shown in Figure \parttworef{fig:Figure3}. 
Table \ref{S-tbl:TableS1} specifies the equivalent layers associated with Figures 
\ref{S-fig:FigureS1}--\ref{S-fig:FigureS18}.

In Figures \ref{S-fig:FigureS1}--\ref{S-fig:FigureS18}, the black line represents the projection of the model on the 
$xy$ plane. The green and black arrows represent, respectively, the projections of the constant main field and 
synthetic source (Figure \parttworef{fig:Figure2}) magnetization directions onto the plane $xy$.
